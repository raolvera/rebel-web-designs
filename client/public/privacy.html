Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

Rebel Web Designs (rebelwebdesigns.com) is a small design studio site. This page explains what happens with your information when you visit. The short version: we don't run ads or analytics, there are no accounts or forms, and we don't sell or share anything about you.

What this site stores

  • No cookies of our own.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising.
  • No sign-ups, forms, or payments on the site right now.

Other services this site uses

Some parts of the page are loaded from other companies. When your browser loads them, those companies receive normal technical details such as your IP address and browser type, and they handle that under their own privacy policies.

  • Media Player (YouTube). The media player uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, and YouTube isn't loaded at all until you use the player (point at it, tap it, press Play, or turn on the video). From then on, YouTube may set its own cookies. Google Privacy Policy
  • Star Wars art cards (X). The artwork images are loaded from X (Twitter), and each card links to the original post and its creator. X Privacy Policy
  • Fonts (Google Fonts). The site's fonts are loaded from Google. Google Fonts privacy FAQ

Your choices

If you'd rather not load YouTube, just don't use the music player. You can also block or clear cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

When the full site launches (with contact, chat, or checkout features), this page will be updated to cover them, and the date above will change.
